# Methodological references

These are the main methodological references behind the model families and forecast-loss interpretation used in the report.

1. Corsi, F. (2009). *A Simple Approximate Long-Memory Model of Realized Volatility*. Journal of Financial Econometrics, 7(2), 174-196. DOI: 10.1093/jjfinec/nbp001.
2. Shephard, N., & Sheppard, K. (2010). *Realising the Future: Forecasting with High-Frequency-Based Volatility (HEAVY) Models*. Journal of Applied Econometrics, 25(2), 197-231. DOI: 10.1002/jae.1158.
3. Patton, A. J. (2011). *Volatility Forecast Comparison Using Imperfect Volatility Proxies*. Journal of Econometrics, 160(1), 246-256. DOI: 10.1016/j.jeconom.2010.03.034.
4. Hansen, P. R., Huang, Z., & Shek, H. H. (2012). *Realized GARCH: A Joint Model for Returns and Realized Measures of Volatility*. Journal of Applied Econometrics, 27(6), 877-906. DOI: 10.1002/jae.1234.
5. Bollerslev, T., Patton, A. J., & Quaedvlieg, R. (2016). *Exploiting the Errors: A Simple Approach for Improved Volatility Forecasting*. Journal of Econometrics, 192(1), 1-18. DOI: 10.1016/j.jeconom.2015.10.007.

The report's empirical claims come from the repository's saved results, not from these papers.
